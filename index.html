      Questions

      Is ScamOrNot a guarantee?

      No — and beware any tool that promises one. ScamOrNot checks for the known patterns real fraud runs on and explains what it finds. A clean result means no strong patterns were detected, not that something is certainly safe. Always verify money decisions through official channels.

      Do you store what I paste?

      No. The checker runs 100% in your browser; nothing you paste is uploaded, logged or stored. The free daily counter lives only on your own device.

      I already sent money. What now?

      Act fast: contact your bank or exchange immediately and report the fraud — speed matters for any chance of a freeze. Report the message to your local police or national fraud line, and warn the group or person who sent it to you. Do not pay any "recovery agent" who contacts you afterwards — recovery scams target victims twice.
